What it does

People now find websites two ways: by searching, and by asking an AI assistant like ChatGPT or Claude. siteseo looks at both.

Checks your whole site

Missing titles, broken links, slow pages, pages Google can't index. 168 checks, each one backed by a published rule you can look up yourself.

Shows if AI assistants can see you

A setting at your web host can block AI assistants without you knowing. siteseo tests what each assistant actually gets when it visits.

Puts the biggest wins first

Connect your free Google Search Console and Analytics, and fixes are ranked by how many real visitors they affect, so you start where it counts.

Catches mistakes before they go live

A quick check runs before you publish and stops the update if it finds a serious problem. It costs nothing and needs no account.

What it costs

The checks and the data from Google and Bing cost nothing. A few extras use paid services, and those never run until you set a monthly limit and say yes.

Free
  • Every check on your site
  • Your Google Search Console and Analytics data
  • Page speed and real-visitor speed from Google
  • Your Bing data, including when Microsoft Copilot cites you
  • The check before you publish
Optional, pay as you go
  • Asking ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity your customers' questions to see if they mention you
  • Keyword and competitor research

You pay those services directly. siteseo shows the cost first, waits for your go-ahead, and stops at the monthly limit you set.

Get started

siteseo runs inside Claude Code, Anthropic's coding assistant. Setup takes a few minutes.

  1. Have Claude Code

    If you don't have it yet, Anthropic's guide shows how to install it. It comes with a Claude subscription.

  2. Add siteseo

    In Claude Code, paste these two lines.

    /plugin marketplace add dtsoden/siteseo
/plugin install siteseo@dtsoden-siteseo

  3. Run it on your website

    Open your website's project in Claude Code and type this. siteseo asks a few questions about your site, then gives you your first report.

    /siteseo

Want your Google, Bing and Analytics data in the report? The step-by-step setup guide walks you through each one.

Questions

Do I need to be a programmer?

No. You talk to siteseo in plain English inside Claude Code, and it explains every problem it finds. It works best with your website's files on your computer, so it can check changes before you publish and make fixes for you.

Will it change my website without asking?

No. It shows you each change first and writes nothing until you say so. It never rewrites your words. Titles, descriptions and page text stay yours, and it only suggests what to change.

How do I get updates?

In Claude Code, run /plugin, open the Installed tab, choose siteseo and select Update now. The latest version and what changed are always listed on the releases page.

Can it check a competitor's website?

It checks websites you own. It doesn't guess at other sites' traffic, because nobody can measure that from outside. Paid competitor research is available if you want it.

Is it really free?

Yes. siteseo is open source under the MIT license. You need Claude Code, and the optional extras above are paid to those services directly.
